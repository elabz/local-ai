SERVING DEFAULTS
  Keep one slot and full GPU offload.
  Start with batch/ubatch 128/64; benchmark 128/128, 256/64, and 256/128.
  Keep Q8 KV by default. Test Q4 KV only to rescue an otherwise passing model.
  Preserve byte-stable system/character prefixes and verify cache hits.
  Pin runtime and GGUF revision/digest. Measure cold and warm paths separately.
  Do not assume cache-reuse=256 means 256 slots; --parallel controls slots.
  Do not add chat KV-cache flags to embedding servers expecting major savings.
